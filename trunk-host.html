Host expectations

  • Keep themes and decorations appropriate for an elementary school audience.
  • Provide your own decorations, candy, or approved giveaway items unless the PTA makes another arrangement with you.
  • No realistic weapons, gore, alcohol, tobacco/vape branding, political campaigning, or adult-only content.
  • Stay within your assigned parking space and keep traffic lanes, sidewalks, and fire access clear.
  • Vehicles must be parked and fully set up before families enter the Trunk-or-Treat area and must remain parked during the activity.
  • Candy and giveaway items should be individually wrapped or safely packaged.
  • Electrical access is limited and must be requested in advance; a request does not guarantee availability.
  • Final placement and participation are subject to PTA/school approval and available space.
